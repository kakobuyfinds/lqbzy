03 / Kakobuy shopping guide

Make the shortlist
work harder.

A long list is only useful when its details are comparable. Keep a small set of clear fields and record what remains unverified.

01

Choose the wardrobe gap first

Start with shoes, a layer or an accessory you need. Use CAFANS categories to narrow discovery before adding individual products.

02

Record the exact variant

Save the product URL, seller, color, size and date checked. Different variants can have different prices and availability.

03

Separate evidence from assumptions

Record measurements, material information and available photos. Mark missing details as unknown instead of guessing.

04

Check the complete cost

Confirm applicable delivery costs, service fees, destination charges and return terms before placing an order.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product shortlist used to organize shopping finds. Useful fields include the product link, category, seller, selected size, listed price and review notes. A spreadsheet is a discovery tool, not a guarantee of quality or availability.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a shopping guide and links to CAFANS product categories. You can create your own spreadsheet using the comparison fields described here.

How should I compare shoes and clothing?

For shoes, compare foot length, the seller’s size chart, materials and available product photos. For clothing, compare garment measurements, fabric information and fit notes rather than relying only on a size label.

Does a listed price include shipping and import charges?

Do not assume that it does. Check the seller or service for applicable domestic shipping, service fees, international shipping and destination taxes or duties before ordering.

Is this an official Kakobuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide linking to CAFANS. Brand references and illustrations do not establish affiliation, endorsement, product availability or authenticity.
